It also explains the distinction between information we process for our own purposes and information we process on behalf of organisations using Connected AF.

2.What Is Connected AF?

Connected AF is a two-way messaging platform that enables organisations to communicate with their supporters and other contacts through SMS, RCS and WhatsApp.

Depending on the services being used, Connected AF may enable organisations to:

  • send and receive messages;
  • manage individual conversations;
  • send messages at scale;
  • create automated messaging journeys;
  • manage communications across campaigns;
  • connect information from fundraising and other third-party systems; and
  • use AI-assisted tools to help users respond to messages.

The information we collect depends on how you interact with Connected AF.

If you create an account, purchase Connected AF, request a demonstration or otherwise enquire about our services, we may collect:

  • job title;

Organisations using Connected AF may upload, integrate or otherwise provide personal information about their supporters or other contacts.

In these circumstances, the organisation using Connected AF will generally be the data controller and Connected AF will process the information on its behalf.

6.SMS, RCS and WhatsApp Communications

Connected AF enables organisations to communicate with individuals using SMS, RCS and WhatsApp.

The organisation sending the communication is responsible for ensuring that its use of these channels complies with applicable privacy, data protection and electronic communications laws and that it has an appropriate lawful basis or permission for the communication where required.

Connected AF may process information necessary to send, receive and manage these communications on behalf of its customers.

This may include:

  • telephone numbers;
  • message content;
  • sender and recipient information;
  • delivery information;
  • timestamps;
  • read or engagement information where supported by the relevant channel;
  • opt-out information; and
  • conversation history.

Where applicable, Connected AF may process opt-out and communication preference information on behalf of customers to help ensure those preferences are respected.

When an organisation connects its WhatsApp Business Account to Connected AF through Meta’s signup process, we receive and store the identifiers for that account and its phone numbers, and the business profile details needed to send and receive WhatsApp messages on the organisation’s behalf. We use this information only to provide messaging services to that organisation. The organisation can disconnect its account at any time, and can ask us to delete this information by contacting support@connectedaf.io.

Customers remain responsible for determining which communications should be sent and to whom.

7.AI-Assisted Features

Connected AF may provide AI-assisted tools designed to help users respond to incoming messages.

For example, when a supporter sends a message, relevant message content and conversation context may be processed using technology provided by OpenAI to generate a suggested response.

The AI acts as a co-pilot rather than an autonomous responder.

The Connected AF user remains in control and may:

  • use the suggested response;
  • edit it before sending;
  • disregard it; or
  • write their own response.

AI-generated suggestions are not automatically sent to supporters.

Connected AF does not intend these features to make solely automated decisions about individuals that have legal or similarly significant effects.

We will maintain appropriate contractual and data protection arrangements with AI providers used to provide these services.

These include:

  • Meta - WhatsApp and related messaging services; and
  • OpenAI - AI-assisted functionality.

We may also use other suppliers where necessary to provide areas such as payment processing, security, authentication, customer support and other technical services.

Where suppliers process personal information on our behalf, we require appropriate contractual and data protection safeguards.

We may also disclose information where required by law, regulation or court order, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend our legal rights.

9.Data Location and International Transfers

Connected AF's primary platform infrastructure and data storage are hosted in the United Kingdom.

Some of the third-party services required to provide Connected AF, including messaging and AI services, may process or transfer personal information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK data protection law.

Depending on the destination and circumstances, these may include UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to approved Standard Contractual Clauses, or other legally recognised safeguards.

Further information about relevant sub-processors and international transfers may be made available through our Data Processing Agreement or sub-processor information.

10.How Long We Keep Information

Organisations using Connected AF can manage how long supporter and messaging information is retained within their account while they have an active subscription. As the data controller, the organisation is responsible for determining an appropriate retention period and ensuring personal information is not retained for longer than necessary.

Customers can delete supporter records and conversation data from their Connected AF account during their subscription.

At the end of a subscription, customer data will be retained for up to 28 days, allowing time for appropriate account closure and, where applicable, the retrieval of data.

Following this 28-day period, the organisation's customer data will be securely deleted from Connected AF's active systems, subject to any information we are required to retain by law and any limited data that may remain temporarily within secure backups in accordance with our backup and deletion procedures.

Customer account, billing and contractual information may be retained separately for longer where necessary to meet our legal, accounting, regulatory or legitimate business requirements.

To request the deletion of your data, please contact support@connectedaf.io
